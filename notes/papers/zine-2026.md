# Zine, Coignion et al. 2026 — Attention to Detail: Energy, Performance and Accuracy Trade-offs Across vLLM Configurations

N. Zine, T. Coignion, V. Stoico, I. Malavolta, C. Quinton, R. Rouvoy, P. Lago.
arXiv:2607.09172 (v2, 17 Jul 2026) · `papers/2607.09172v2.pdf`
Owner: Lena

## In short

A controlled full-factorial study of three vLLM options: attention kernel
(FlashAttention-2/3, FlashInfer), **prefix caching** (on/off) and chunked prefill
(on/off). It covers 5 models × 5 tasks × 30 repetitions = 9,000 runs and
measures energy, latency/TTFT and accuracy. The attention kernel and prefix
caching matter, chunked prefill hardly matters, and the choice of model
dominates everything.

## Setup

- Models: Qwen3-32B, Qwen3-4B, Magistral-Small-24B, Llama-3.1-8B, Llama-3.2-3B.
- Tasks (Table II): AssistantTraces (10-turn coding sessions), EvoEval, LongBench-v2
  (≈25k-token prompts), Natural Questions, **WildChat (200 conversations, cut
  to 5 turns)**.
- Multi-turn runs send one batch per turn, with one prompt per active
  conversation, so they are batched across conversations. The history uses the
  dataset's original assistant replies.
- Energy is CPU (`perf`) + GPU (`nvidia-smi`) at 1 Hz, E = mean power × time.
  A 30 s idle calibration is run, but the paper does not clearly say whether
  idle power is subtracted. The cache is reset between runs.
- Sampling at temperature 0.4, top-p 0.95, max 2048 tokens. vLLM 0.10.2,
  4 × A100-40GB nodes.
- Statistics: Shapiro-Wilk, ART ANOVA, Holm-Bonferroni correction, Cliff's δ,
  and a Pareto front.

## Key findings

1. **The attention kernel and prefix caching are the main levers.** Chunked
   prefill only triggers above 8,192 batched tokens, so it barely mattered
   (Table III, §VI).
2. **Prefix caching reduces energy in most cases, but only a little and
   depending on the task** (Fig. 4). On WildChat the reduction is 0.8–15.7 kJ
   per run depending on the model. For Llama-3B that is about 1–2 % of about
   57–66 kJ (Table V).
3. It can also **increase** energy: Llama-3B on AssistantTraces used +3.56 kJ
   with caching enabled.
4. **TTFT reacts much more strongly than energy.** Across configurations the
   median differences reach 324 % for TTFT but 38.8 % for energy (§V-A).
5. **Enabling caching has effects even without cache hits.** Qwen-32B on
   LongBench saved energy although LongBench has no shared prefixes. The
   authors suspect a different KV-block allocation path.
6. **Configuration options can change accuracy.** Prefix caching significantly
   shifted accuracy for 6 model–benchmark pairs. The likely cause is
   floating-point reduction order combined with sampling.
7. **Model choice dominates the energy–latency–accuracy Pareto front.**
   Configuration options only give local improvements.

## Limitations

- Only one GPU type (A100). The authors note that FA-3 is tuned for H100.
- They used the offline batch interface, not the HTTP server, so caching
  behaviour under real arrival patterns may differ.
- `nvidia-smi` on A100 samples only about 25 % of runtime, which can cause up to
  65 % error on spiky loads (cites Yang et al., SC24).
- Accuracy is checked only on EvoEval and LongBench. The multi-turn tasks have
  no quality metric.
- Multi-turn coverage is short (5–10 turns) and batched across conversations.
  Because the history uses the dataset's replies rather than the model's own,
  the cached prefix from the previous turn is not fully reused. (Our reading.)

## What it means for our project

- This is the closest related work. It already shows that caching gives modest
  savings in short multi-turn chat. Our contribution has to be what it leaves
  out: **long conversations, per-turn growth, a single user vs. concurrent
  users, context strategies, and quality on history-dependent questions**.
- Finding 2 supports our hypothesis that energy savings ≪ token savings.
- Finding 6 means we measure quality even for "pure efficiency" settings, use
  greedy decoding, and report the full vLLM config.
- We can reuse their method: randomised order, many repetitions, ART ANOVA +
  Cliff's δ, idle calibration, cache reset between runs.
- Llama-3.2-3B and Qwen3-4B fit on our 16 GB GPUs, so we can use the same
  models for comparability.
