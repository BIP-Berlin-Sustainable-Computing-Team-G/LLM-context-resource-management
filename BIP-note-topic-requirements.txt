PRESENTATION:
TITLE: context windows and KV-cache reuse
In a chat, the whole conversation history is sent again with every new message. key-value
(KV) cache can be used to avoid re-computation but it is a limited resource.
Overarching Research Question:
How much energy can context management and KV-cache reuse save in multi-turn
conversations, and what does it cost in answer quality?
Possible Research Questions:
• How does energy per turn grow with conversation length when the full history is resent,
with and without cache reuse?
• How do context strategies (full history, sliding window, summarised history) compare in
energy and answer quality?
• Do API prompt caching and energy estimation tools agree: does EcoLogits account for
cached tokens?
